# Listening history and poster wall

Open **Listening history** from Home and enable recording. It is off by default.

Lyricon records tracks received from lyric providers, independently of app screens or status-bar lyric visibility. Titles, artists, albums, player sources, actual listening time and available covers are kept in the app's local database. Text-only providers can be recorded when media metadata is available. Disabling recording retains existing history and stops new records; earlier plays are not imported.

History includes short plays. Rankings count a play after **half the track or four minutes**, whichever is shorter; an unknown duration uses four minutes. Pauses and seeks do not add listening time. Resume continues the same session. Duplicate song callbacks do not add plays; natural track repeats create new sessions.

The filter to the left of the top-right trash icon opens a Miuix grouped dropdown with time and player options separated by a divider. Time options include today, seven days, thirty days, one year and all time; the defaults are all time and all players. The menu stays open after selection so both filters can be adjusted together, with haptic feedback when opening and selecting. Both filters apply to stats, history and the poster wall. A standard Miuix TabRow switches the three pages, while poster periods use a contour TabRow. Statistics include qualified plays, actual listening minutes, unique tracks, daily trends and track/artist/album rankings. Track and album rankings display local covers, falling back to a music icon. Listening across midnight is split by local date; a play is counted on its starting date.

The poster wall uses Halcyon's mosaic geometry. A contour Miuix TabRow selects the **last 12 hours, one day, two days or three days**, defaulting to one day. These rolling 12-, 24-, 48- or 72-hour windows intersect with the toolbar time range and player filter. Only qualified plays started in that interval contribute to the wall, which displays up to 96 tracks in ranking order. The rolling range refreshes every minute while the wall is open. Below-threshold plays remain in history and are excluded from the wall. Pinch to zoom and drag to browse, or tap **Full screen** for an immersive view; use Close or Back to exit. Missing covers become typographic posters using real track metadata. **Save PNG** exports the complete wall to a selected location; **Share poster wall** opens the system share sheet with temporary read access to the generated PNG.

The Miuix trash icon in the toolbar opens a confirmation dialog with a warning-colored confirmation button. Confirming deletes local records and covers, retaining the recording setting. This feature does not upload scrobbles to online services. Settings backups do not contain the listening database. Restart System UI after upgrading to load the recorder.
